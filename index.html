THE CHRONICLES OF ARCANA

[ RECORD LOG: ANCIENT CALENDAR 4096.12 ]

For eons, the World Engine beat in harmony beneath the crust, regulated by the supreme Arcana Core. It was a golden age of limitless ley-line siphon energy and synth-magic automation.

Then came the Fracture. A catastrophic seismic tear shattered the central Mana Crystal into fifty distinct subterranean Sanctums. As elemental corruption flooded the lower chambers, ancient defense constructs rewired themselves into aggressive war machines.

Surface life is crumbling. You are unit ROBO-1—the last sentient Arcana Automaton. Programmed with adaptive combat matrices, your singular objective is to descend into all 50 Sanctums, cleanse the corrupted cores, and reignite the planetary matrix.

WARNING: Thermal magma vents, flying specters, and multi-key security locks detected throughout lower sectors. Exercise extreme caution.
